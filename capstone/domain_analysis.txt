Circuit Breaker
- Protect the grid - isolate the problem
	Fault causes:
		Mechanical wear, contact degradation, control failures
Substation
	- Substation acts like a traffic junction for electricity

Functions:
	Voltage transformation
	Power routing
	Fault isolation
	Grid monitoring

Switchgear
	- Control and isolate electrical equipment. You can think of switchgear as the "traffic controller" of the grid.
	
	Failure Causes
		Arc flash
		Insulation failure
		Aging components

Transformer
	- Transformers change voltage levels.

	Failure Causes
		Insulation degradation
		Oil contamination
		Overheating
		Moisture ingress
		Overloading

Transmission Line 
	- Carry electricity over long distances.
	Failure Causes
		Storms
		Lightning
		Tree contact
		Conductor damage
		Tower collapse
